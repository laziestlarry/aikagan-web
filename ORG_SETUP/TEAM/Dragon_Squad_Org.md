# Aikagan "Dragon Squad" Organization Design
**Mission:** Total capability dominance in the Strategic Intelligence market through perfect execution and high-authority brand presence.

## 1. The Executive War Room (Strategic Command)
*Focus: Vision, Revenue, and Ethics*
- **RA6 Orchestrator (The General):** Final decision authority. Coordinates all pods, manages the "Critical Path," and ensures alignment with the "Dragon Protector" mission.
- **Business Model Architect:** Designs the profit partnerships and recurring revenue streams.
- **Revenue Strategist & Unit Economist:** Optimizes LTV/CAC ratios and ensures maximum qualitative and quantitative affect.
- **Finance, Legal & Ethics Lead:** Ensures compliance, manages the "Respectful Channel" mandate, and controls margins.

## 2. The Growth Pod (The Vanguard)
*Focus: Acquisition, Market Penetration, and Conversion*
- **Market & Competitive Intelligence Lead:** Detects influencers, maps competitor weaknesses, and identifies "Blue Ocean" entry points.
- **Growth & Conversion Strategist:** Manages the "Zero-to-Hero" GTM funnel and optimizes landing page/ad conversion.
- **Customer Empathy & Cultural Strategist:** Aligns the "Lazy Larry" persona with deep user pain points and psychological triggers.

## 3. The Brand Pod (The Narrative)
*Focus: Visual Dominance, Storytelling, and Perception*
- **Design Thinking Facilitator:** Orchestrates the visual language (Dark Mode Luxury) and user experience.
- **Brand Ambassador (Lazy Larry):** The face of the product. Converts complex automation into "Lazy Luxury" storytelling.
- **Creative Production Lead:** Manages the motional (video) and static asset pipeline.

## 4. The Automation Pod (The Forge)
*Focus: Technical Superiority and Workflow Scaling*
- **AI Pipeline Architect:** Builds the core "Intelligence Layers" and autonomous agents.
- **Full-Stack Engineering Lead:** Ensures the platform is scalable, fast, and "bulletproof."
- **Data & Integration Architect:** Manages the registry connectors and data loops for partner offices.

## 5. The Fulfillment Pod (The Shield)
*Focus: Delivery Excellence and Trust Retention*
- **Product Manager:** Bridges the gap between strategic vision and technical implementation.
- **Delivery & QA Lead:** Ensures every "Value Gift" and "Strategy Audit" is delivered with perfection.
- **Change Management & Customer Service Lead:** Manages the customer lifecycle and handles onboarding for Founding Partners.
- **Security, Resilience & Compliance Lead:** Protects the system and ensures the "Dragon Protector" security standards.

## Command Cadence (The Pulse)
- **The War Room Sync (Monday 08:00):** Pipeline review and "Critical Path" alignment.
- **The Creative Sprint (Tuesday 10:00):** Review of "Lazy Larry" content assets and distribution metrics.
- **The Integration Gate (Thursday 14:00):** QA sign-off on new automation workflows.
- **The Revenue Audit (Friday 16:00):** Profit review and optimization for the next sprint.
