# Aikagan Workflow Orchestration Engine
**Philosophy:** Maximum throughput with zero friction. Delegation is absolute; verification is systemic.

## 1. The Master Value Chain (MVC)
The path from "Cold Lead" to "Enterprise Partner."

**Stage A: Attraction (The Magnet)**
- **Trigger:** Lazy Larry Social Content / AI Registry Listing.
- **Lead Owner:** Growth Pod.
- **Gate 1 (Interest):** User downloads "Strategic Operator's Toolkit."
- **Output:** Lead captured in CRM $\rightarrow$ Triggered "Nurture Sequence."

**Stage B: Qualification (The Filter)**
- **Trigger:** User requests "Free Strategy Audit" or applies for "Founding Partner."
- **Lead Owner:** Growth Pod $\rightarrow$ Fulfillment Pod.
- **Gate 2 (Intent):** 15-min qualification call (Filtered by the "Customer Empathy" strategist).
- **Output:** Qualified Prospect $\rightarrow$ Strategic Opportunity Map.

**Stage C: Conversion (The Close)**
- **Trigger:** Delivery of the "Custom AI Opportunity Map."
- **Lead Owner:** Revenue Strategist $\rightarrow$ RA6 Orchestrator.
- **Gate 3 (Commitment):** Proposal sign-off / Deposit payment.
- **Output:** New Client/Partner $\rightarrow$ Onboarding Trigger.

**Stage D: Delivery (The Perfection)**
- **Trigger:** Onboarding Form Completion.
- **Lead Owner:** Fulfillment Pod $\rightarrow$ Automation Pod.
- **Gate 4 (Quality):** QA Lead sign-off on "First Value Delivery" (The first automated win).
- **Output:** Case Study Material $\rightarrow$ Feedback Loop.

**Stage E: Expansion (The Dominance)**
- **Trigger:** 30-day success review.
- **Lead Owner:** Revenue Strategist $\rightarrow$ Business Model Architect.
- **Gate 5 (Loyalty):** Upgrade to Enterprise / Refer 3 Partners.
- **Output:** Recurring Revenue $\rightarrow$ Brand Ambassador testimonial.

## 3. The "Fail-Safe" Loop
Every gate has a **Reversion Trigger**. If a lead fails Gate 2 (Qualification), they are not discarded; they are sent back to "The Magnet" stage with a different "Value Gift" tailored to their specific failure reason.
