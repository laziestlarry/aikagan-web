# Aikagan Strategic Sessions & Gates
**Goal:** Ensure "perfection by design" and prevent "movement without arrival."

## 1. The Engineering Gate System (The "Quality Wall")
No asset or workflow moves to production without passing its respective gate.

| Gate | Name | Requirement | Sign-off Authority | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **G1** | **The Strategic Fit** | Does this content align with "Lazy Larry" and the "Dragon Protector" mission? | Brand Lead | Go/No-Go to Design |
| **G2** | **The Value Threshold** | Does this "Free Offer" provide genuine, non-obvious value? | Growth Lead | Go/No-Go to Production |
| **G3** | **The Technical Zero** | Is the automation bug-free and scalable? | QA Lead | Go/No-Go to Deployment |
| **G4** | **The Trust Audit** | Is the communication respectful and transparent? | Finance/Legal Lead | Go/No-Go to Outreach |
| **G5** | **The Outcome Verify** | Did the delivery result in a measurable "Win" for the client? | RA6 Orchestrator | Entry into Portfolio |

## 2. The Strategic Session Cadence
Designed for high-velocity, low-noise communication.

### A. The "War Room" (Weekly Sync)
- **Duration:** 45 mins.
- **Focus:** The Critical Path.
- **Agenda:** 
    1. Review G5 (Portfolio Wins) from previous week.
    2. Identify bottlenecks in the G1-G4 pipeline.
    3. Re-allocate resources for the next Sprint.

### B. The "Creative Forge" (Bi-Weekly Production)
- **Duration:** 60 mins.
- **Focus:** Lazy Larry Narrative expansion.
- **Agenda:**
    1. Storyboard review for next 2 weeks of content.
    2. A/B test analysis (Which "Lazy" angle is converting best?).
    3. Production of "Motional" assets.

### C. The "Integration Deep-Dive" (Ad-hoc/On-demand)
- **Duration:** 90 mins.
- **Focus:** Solving a specific technical or strategic block.
- **Agenda:**
    1. Problem definition.
    2. "Dragon Protector" solve (Analyze $\rightarrow$ Automate $\rightarrow$ Accelerate).
    3. Immediate implementation of the fix.
