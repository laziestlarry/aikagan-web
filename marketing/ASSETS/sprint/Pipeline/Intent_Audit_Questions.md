# THE FOUNDING PARTNER INTENT AUDIT
## Objective: Filter for High-Intent, High-Authority Operators

To move a lead from "The Magnet" to "The Close", they must answer these 3 high-leverage questions:

1. **The Friction Question:** "What is the single biggest manual bottleneck in your current delivery process that costs you the most time or revenue per month?"
   * *Goal: Identify the 'Pain-Killer' opportunity.*

2. **The Authority Question:** "On a scale of 1-10, how integrated is AI into your core business operations (not just as a tool, but as an autonomous process)?"
   * *Goal: Determine the 'Awareness Gap' and potential for a 'Zero-Gap' upgrade.*

3. **The Commitment Question:** "If we can prove a 30% increase in your delivery velocity through a zero-gap pipeline, are you positioned to activate a partnership in the next 14 days?"
   * *Goal: Filter for urgency and decision-making power.*
