# AI-Driven Influencer & Partner Detection

## 1. The "Surgical Scan" Method
Instead of searching for "Influencers," we search for "Problem-Solvers."
- **Step A (Keyword Clustering):** Use AI to generate 50 long-tail keywords related to "Strategic Bottlenecks" (e.g., "agency scaling pain," "market research fatigue").
- **Step B (Social Scraping):** Use tools like Perplexity or specialized scrapers to find who is consistently answering high-value questions on Reddit, LinkedIn, and X using these keywords.
- **Step C (Authority Scoring):** Rank potential partners by:
    - **Engagement Rate:** Do people actually follow their advice?
    - **Sentiment:** Is the community respecting their expertise or just their fame?
    - **Alignment:** Does their "Vibe" match the "Dragon Protector" aesthetic?

## 2. The "Bridge" Approach
Find influencers who are *almost* our target but are missing the "Execution" piece.
- **Target:** The "AI News" influencers (who tell people *what* tools exist).
- **The Pitch:** "You tell them what's new; we provide the system that makes it actually work for their business."
- **The Result:** A symbiotic relationship where they remain the news source and we become the "Implementation Engine."
