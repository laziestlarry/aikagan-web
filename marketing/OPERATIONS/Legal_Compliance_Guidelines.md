# Aikagan Legal & Respectful Outreach Guidelines

## 1. The "Value-First" Mandate
To avoid being flagged as spam and to maintain high-brand authority:
- **No Cold-Selling:** Never lead with a price or a "buy now" link.
- **Permission-Based Marketing:** Always ask "Would you be open to..." or "May I send you..." before providing links.
- **The 3-Message Rule:** Max 3 follow-ups. If no response, move to the "Nurture" list or archive.

## 2. Platform Compliance
- **Facebook/Instagram:** Avoid "Engagement Bait" (e.g., "LIKE THIS POST TO WIN"). Instead, use "Value-Driven Invitations."
- **LinkedIn:** Use personalized connection requests. Avoid automated "bot" messages that use generic templates.
- **Email:** Include a clear "Unsubscribe" link and a physical address (or registered business address) to comply with CAN-SPAM and GDPR.

## 3. Partnership Ethics
- **Transparency:** Clearly state the revenue share to partners.
- **Data Privacy:** Ensure all partner-collected leads are handled according to GDPR/CCPA standards.
- **Honesty in Claims:** Never promise "Guaranteed Income." Promise "Strategic Efficiency" and "Systemic Growth."
