# Aikagan Profit Partnership Model
**Goal:** Leverage existing networks to accelerate income and distribution without high CAC.

## 1. Sales Office Partnerships (B2B)
**Target:** Marketing agencies, business consultancies, and digital transformation firms.
**The Offer:** "The Strategic Intelligence Layer."
- **Revenue Share:** 25% recurring commission on all client seats brought in.
- **Value Proposition:** The agency adds Aikagan to their service stack, increasing their delivery speed and outcome quality, while earning a passive stream.
- **Incentive:** "Gold Tier" status for offices bringing in >$5k MRR, granting them a dedicated account manager and custom feature requests.

## 2. Individual Affiliate Partnerships (B2C/Prosumer)
**Target:** Freelancers, AI enthusiasts, and productivity coaches.
**The Offer:** "The Growth Partner Program."
- **Commission:** 15% per referral.
- **The "Hero" Path:** Affiliates who reach 50 referrals are upgraded to "Founding Ambassadors," receiving a share of the overall platform growth fund (performance-based bonus).
- **Tools Provided:** Pre-made content kits, landing page templates, and a unique "Fast-Track" code for their audience.

## 3. Trust-Building Mechanics
- **Escrow/Transparency:** Transparent dashboard for partners to track their referrals and earnings in real-time.
- **Quality Control:** Partners are trained via a "Dragon Protector Certification" (mini-course) to ensure they represent the value proposition accurately.
