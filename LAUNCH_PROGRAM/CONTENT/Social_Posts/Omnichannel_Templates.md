# Aikagan Omnichannel Content Kit

## 1. YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels (The "Aha!" Moments)
**Format:** 15-60 seconds. Fast cuts. Captions.
- **Hook:** "Stop doing [Manual Task] like it's 2023."
- **The Gap:** Show a screen recording of a tedious process (e.g., manual market research).
- **The Solve:** Transition to Aikagan. Show the "Magic Moment" (AI generating the strategy/automation).
- **CTA:** "Get the Strategic Operator's Toolkit for free. Link in bio."

## 2. Instagram (The "Authority" Aesthetic)
**Format:** Carousel (5-7 slides).
- **Slide 1:** "The 3 Hidden Levers of Strategic Income." (Strong visual)
- **Slide 2-4:** Break down a specific framework (e.g., The Dragon Protector Framework: Analyze -> Automate -> Accelerate).
- **Slide 5:** Case study snippet: "How [Client] saved 40 hours/week."
- **Slide 6:** "Want this system? Comment 'DRAGON' for the free toolkit."
- **Slide 7:** Call to Action (Link in bio).

## 3. Facebook (The "Community" Conversation)
**Format:** Long-form post with a valuable image/infographic.
- **Opening:** "Is anyone else feeling the AI fatigue? Too many tools, not enough results."
- **Body:** Explain the difference between "AI tools" and "Strategic Intelligence." Position Aikagan as the orchestrator.
- **The Offer:** "I'm giving away my Strategic Operator's Toolkit to the first 50 people who want to actually build a system, not just prompt a bot."
- **CTA:** "Drop a 'YES' below and I'll DM it to you."

## 4. Pinterest (The "Knowledge" Map)
**Format:** Vertical Infographic.
- **Title:** "The Zero-to-Hero GTM Roadmap for AI-Driven Businesses."
- **Content:** A clean flow-chart showing the steps from Idea -> Infrastructure -> Distribution -> Revenue.
- **CTA:** "Full Guide available at Aikagan.web"
