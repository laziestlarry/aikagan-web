## Bid decision rule

Bid when all are true:
1. You can produce the requested native format or a buyer-accepted alternative.
2. You can show relevant proof or create a legitimate labeled sample without misrepresentation.
3. The first milestone can be delivered within the client's urgency window.
4. The expected payout materially exceeds platform fees and any required software cost.
5. Regulated work does not require you to impersonate a licensed architect/engineer or issue approvals you are not legally authorized to issue.

Avoid projects that require unpaid full samples, large purchases before award, off-platform payment before trust is established, credential sharing, remote-access installation from unknown clients, or impossible deadlines.
