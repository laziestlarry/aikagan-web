# AIKAGAN Free Lab — Feedback Reach Campaign

Primary URL: https://aikagan.com/tools/
Feedback URL: https://aikagan.com/feedback/

## Campaign objective
Maximize qualified trial of the free tools and collect concrete feedback about usefulness, missing capabilities, desired outcomes, and blockers. Do not optimize for vanity impressions or ask for artificial votes.

## Core message
We changed AIKAGAN from “buy a pack first” to “use something useful first.” The Free Lab is public: Revenue Leak Scan, Golden Delivery Sample, and AutonomaX Explorer. Try one, then tell us what created value, what failed, and what you would actually want implemented.

## Feedback questions
1. What did you try?
2. What was immediately useful?
3. What was confusing or missing?
4. What outcome would make this genuinely valuable?
5. What currently stops you from solving it?

## KPI hierarchy
1. Tool starts
2. Tool completions
3. Feedback submissions
4. Repeat visits
5. Qualified implementation requests
6. Paid conversion only after the above proves demand

## Rules
- Ask for feedback, not votes.
- Do not mass-DM or automate unsolicited promotion.
- Tailor each community post to its rules and audience.
- Reply to every substantive comment with useful context.
- Convert repeated pain points into product backlog evidence.
